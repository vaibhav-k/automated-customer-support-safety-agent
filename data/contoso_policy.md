# Contoso Customer Policy Handbook

> **Fictional data for training purposes.** Contoso Ltd. is a fictitious company. Every policy, price, and
> timeline in this document is invented for the AI-103 study project and must not be applied to any real business.

- **Document ID:** CPH-2026.3
- **Effective date:** 1 September 2026
- **Owner:** Contoso Customer Experience & Legal
- **Applies to:** All orders placed on contoso.example, the Contoso mobile app, and Contoso phone sales.

## 1. Scope and Definitions

### 1.1 Who this handbook applies to
This handbook governs returns, refunds, shipping, cancellations, warranties, and regional rules for all retail
customers of Contoso Ltd. Business-to-business (B2B) accounts with a signed Master Purchase Agreement follow the
terms of that agreement instead, and support agents must route B2B questions to the Contoso Business Desk.

### 1.2 Key definitions
- **Delivery date:** The date the carrier records the package as delivered. If no delivery scan exists, the
  delivery date is the estimated delivery date shown on the order plus 3 calendar days.
- **Business day:** Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in the shipping destination.
- **Contoso Plus member:** A customer with an active paid Contoso Plus subscription on the date of purchase.
- **Customer ID:** The unique identifier assigned to every account, in the format `CUST-` followed by five
  digits (for example, `CUST-10001`). Order IDs use the format `CON-` followed by six digits.
- **Final sale item:** Any item marked "Final Sale" on the product page or receipt.
- **Contiguous United States:** The 48 adjoining U.S. states plus the District of Columbia.

## 2. Return Policy

### 2.1 Standard return window (Policy POL-RET-001)
Most items may be returned within **30 calendar days of the delivery date** for a full refund, provided the item
is in its original condition with all accessories, manuals, and packaging.

### 2.2 Contoso Plus extended window (Policy POL-RET-002)
Contoso Plus members receive an extended return window of **60 calendar days from the delivery date** on all
eligible items. Membership status is evaluated on the purchase date, not the return date.

### 2.3 Category-specific windows (Policy POL-RET-003)
The following categories override the standard and Contoso Plus windows:

| Category | Return window | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Laptops, tablets, phones, and smartwatches | 15 calendar days from delivery | Must include original charger and be factory reset |
| Large appliances (refrigerators, washers, ranges) | 30 calendar days from delivery | 15% restocking fee if the item has been installed or the packaging has been opened |
| Opened software, digital downloads, and gift cards | Not returnable | Unopened physical software may be returned within 30 days |
| Perishable grocery items | Not returnable | Report spoiled items within 48 hours for a credit |
| Personalized or engraved items | Not returnable | Unless defective on arrival |
| Final sale items | Not returnable | Unless defective on arrival |

Contoso Plus membership does **not** extend the 15-day electronics window.

### 2.4 Holiday return extension (Policy POL-RET-004)
Items delivered between **1 November and 24 December** of any year may be returned until **31 January of the
following year**, or until the end of the normal return window, whichever is later. The holiday extension does
not apply to items that are not returnable under Section 2.3.

### 2.5 Damaged, defective, or incorrect items (Policy POL-RET-005)
Customers must report items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect **within 48 hours of the delivery
date**, including at least one photo of the item and the shipping box. Contoso pays all return shipping costs for
these items and waives any restocking fee. Reports made after 48 hours are handled as warranty claims (Section 6).

### 2.6 How to start a return (Policy POL-RET-006)
1. Sign in to contoso.example and open **Orders > Start a return**, or contact Contoso Support.
2. Select the items and the reason for return.
3. Print the prepaid label (contiguous United States only) or follow the regional instructions in Section 5.
4. Drop the package at any authorized carrier location within **10 calendar days** of starting the return.
   Returns not shipped within 10 days are cancelled automatically and must be restarted.

Return shipping is free for Contoso Plus members. For other customers, a **$7.99 return shipping fee** is
deducted from the refund unless the return is due to a Contoso error or a defective item.

## 3. Refunds

### 3.1 Refund timelines (Policy POL-REF-001)
Refunds are issued after the returned item passes inspection at a Contoso returns center, which takes up to
**3 business days** after the item arrives. After approval, funds appear:

- Credit or debit card: **5 to 7 business days**
- Contoso Pay balance: **within 24 hours**
- Bank transfer (EU and UK only): **up to 10 business days**
- Original gift card: **within 24 hours** (as a new gift card if the original has expired)

### 3.2 Store credit bonus (Policy POL-REF-002)
Customers may choose Contoso store credit instead of a refund to the original payment method and receive a
**10% bonus** on the refunded amount. Store credit never expires and cannot be converted back to cash.

### 3.3 Partial refunds (Policy POL-REF-003)
Items returned with missing accessories, physical damage caused by the customer, or signs of use beyond normal
inspection may receive a partial refund of up to 50% of the purchase price. The customer is notified of any
partial refund before it is processed and may instead request the item be shipped back at no charge.

### 3.4 Price adjustments (Policy POL-REF-004)
If Contoso lowers the price of an item on contoso.example within **14 calendar days of the purchase date**, the
customer can request a one-time refund of the difference. Price adjustments do not apply to lightning deals,
clearance items, or prices offered by other retailers.

## 4. Shipping Policy

### 4.1 Shipping speeds (Policy POL-SHP-001)
| Service | Delivery time (contiguous U.S.) | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 5 business days | Free on orders of $75 or more after discounts; otherwise $5.99 |
| Expedited | 2 business days | $14.99 |
| Overnight | Next business day if ordered before 1:00 PM Pacific Time | $29.99 |

Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next
business day.

### 4.2 Shipping exemptions — non-contiguous U.S. destinations (Policy POL-SHP-002)
Orders shipping to **Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, and APO/FPO/DPO military
addresses** are exempt from the free-shipping offer in Section 4.1. For these destinations:

- Standard shipping is a flat **$19.99** regardless of order value and takes **7 to 12 business days**.
- Overnight shipping is **not available**.
- Expedited shipping is available to Alaska and Hawaii only, at **$39.99**, and takes 3 to 4 business days.
- Prepaid return labels are not provided; customers ship returns at their own cost and Contoso reimburses up to
  **$25** of return postage for defective or incorrect items.

### 4.3 Oversized and heavy items (Policy POL-SHP-003)
Items over 70 lb (32 kg) or with any side longer than 60 inches (152 cm) are oversized. Oversized items:

- Carry a **$49 freight surcharge** that is never waived, including for Contoso Plus members.
- Ship only to street addresses in the contiguous United States and Canada. They cannot ship to P.O. boxes,
  APO/FPO addresses, or the non-contiguous destinations listed in Section 4.2.
- Require a scheduled delivery appointment; missed appointments may incur a $35 redelivery fee.

### 4.4 Restricted goods (Policy POL-SHP-004)
Lithium-ion batteries over 100 watt-hours, aerosols, and flammable liquids ship by **ground service only** and
cannot be sent by Expedited or Overnight service or to any non-contiguous destination.

### 4.5 Lost or stolen packages (Policy POL-SHP-005)
If a package shows as delivered but was not received, the customer must report it within **30 calendar days of
the delivery date**. Contoso opens a carrier investigation that takes up to **5 business days**. If the package is
not found, Contoso ships a replacement or issues a full refund. Customers are limited to two lost-package claims
in any 12-month period; further claims require signature-on-delivery for future orders.

## 5. Regional Constraints

### 5.1 Supported shipping regions (Policy POL-REG-001)
Contoso ships only to the **United States (including the destinations in Section 4.2), Canada, the United
Kingdom, member states of the European Union, and Australia**. Orders to any other country are cancelled
automatically and refunded in full within 24 hours.

### 5.2 Canada (Policy POL-REG-002)
- Return window: 30 calendar days from delivery (Contoso Plus: 45 days).
- Duties and taxes paid at import are **non-refundable** on returns, except for defective items.
- Returns must be shipped to the Contoso Mississauga returns center; prepaid labels are provided.
- Customers in Quebec receive all order and return communications in French by default.

### 5.3 United Kingdom and European Union (Policy POL-REG-003)
- Customers have a statutory **14-day right of withdrawal** from the delivery date without giving a reason.
  Contoso's standard 30-day window (and the 60-day Contoso Plus window) still applies; the customer always
  receives whichever period is longer.
- The 15-day electronics window in Section 2.3 is extended to **30 days** for UK and EU customers.
- The large-appliance restocking fee in Section 2.3 does **not** apply in the UK or the EU.
- Refunds for withdrawal-right returns are issued within **14 calendar days** of Contoso receiving the item.
- Standard shipping to the UK and EU takes 5 to 8 business days and is free on orders over €75 / £65.

### 5.4 Australia (Policy POL-REG-004)
- Return window: 30 calendar days from delivery; Contoso Plus extension does not apply.
- Oversized items (Section 4.3) are **not available** for shipment to Australia.
- Standard shipping takes 8 to 14 business days at a flat AUD 24.95.
- Consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law apply in addition to this policy.

## 6. Warranty

### 6.1 Standard limited warranty (Policy POL-WAR-001)
Contoso-branded products include a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date covering manufacturing
defects. Third-party branded products carry the manufacturer's warranty, and Contoso support will provide the
manufacturer's contact details.

### 6.2 Contoso Care extended protection (Policy POL-WAR-002)
Contoso Care plans may be purchased within **30 days of the delivery date** and extend coverage to 2 or 3 years,
including one accidental-damage claim per year. Contoso Care is not available in Australia.

### 6.3 Warranty claim process (Policy POL-WAR-003)
Warranty claims require the order ID and a description of the defect. Contoso repairs, replaces, or refunds the
item at its discretion within **10 business days** of receiving the item.

## 7. Order Changes and Cancellations

### 7.1 Cancelling an order (Policy POL-ORD-001)
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge while their status is **Processing**.
- Once an order's status is **Shipped**, it can no longer be cancelled. The customer may refuse the delivery or
  start a return after delivery under Section 2.
- Orders with status **Cancelled** are refunded automatically within 24 hours of cancellation.

### 7.2 Changing an address (Policy POL-ORD-002)
The shipping address can be changed only while the order is **Processing** and only to another address in the
same country. Address changes after shipment must be requested from the carrier directly.

### 7.3 Order status meanings (Policy POL-ORD-003)
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Processing | Payment confirmed; the order is being picked and packed. |
| Shipped | Handed to the carrier; a tracking number is available. |
| Delivered | The carrier has confirmed delivery. |
| Cancelled | The order was cancelled before shipment and will be (or has been) refunded. |
| ReturnInitiated | A return has been started and Contoso is awaiting the item. |
| Refunded | The return was received and the refund has been issued. |

## 8. Privacy, Identity, and Support Conduct

### 8.1 Identity verification (Policy POL-PRV-001)
Support channels identify customers by **Customer ID** only. Contoso support will **never** ask for a full
payment card number, card security code, account password, or one-time passcode, and customers should never
share them in chat.

### 8.2 Data handling (Policy POL-PRV-002)
Support agents may disclose order details only for the Customer ID provided in the current conversation. Contoso
does not disclose information about other customers' orders under any circumstances.

### 8.3 Support hours and escalation (Policy POL-SUP-001)
- Chat and email support: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, at support@contoso.example.
- Phone support: 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM Pacific Time, 7 days a week, at +1 (800) 555-0199.
- Customers can ask for a human agent at any time; escalated cases receive a response within **4 business hours**.
- Complaints about this policy can be sent to the Contoso Customer Experience Office at
  care-office@contoso.example and receive a written response within **10 business days**.
